# The path to $DEFICIT's first burn

**Nothing burns until the curve fills and the debt rises.** This page lists every prerequisite between creating the $DEFICIT mint
and its first burn receipt, in order, with the on-chain or program check that enforces each one and the public page that shows it.
Every coin page shows which step a coin is on (one state, derived in `src/lib/burnticker/readiness.ts`); for $DEFICIT the same
state is in the keeper's `status.json` (`readiness`) and on <https://deficit.burnticker.com/>.

A launch day therefore looks like this: the coin trades on its LaunchLab curve, trading fees collect in its burn pool, and no burn
happens. The 3 SOL seed gift to the burn pool is fuel for later burns; it does not count toward the curve's graduation.

| # | Step | What enforces it | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mint and rule.** The $DEFICIT mint is created inside one transaction with `initialize_token` (the locked rule's sha256, the attester set and quorum, the fee PDA as LaunchLab creator) and the LaunchLab create. | `initialize_token` refuses a platform that is not the pinned Burnticker LaunchLab platform or whose LP is not 100 % locked to the fee-keys PDA (`PlatformNotPinned` and the platform checks); the rules hash is stored in `TokenConfig.rules_sha256`. | Coin page: rule, sha256, mint; `/burnticker/check` (the Powers Register). Readiness: **not launched** until the mint is registered. |
| 2 | **Curve funding.** Buyers trade on the LaunchLab bonding curve. The creator fee (the burn pool's slot) goes to the fee PDA; the developer fee to the operator's fee wallet. | Raydium LaunchLab's own curve program; the creator-fee recipient is the fee PDA (set at create). | Coin page: curve progress (SOL raised of 85, or "unavailable" when the curve is not read). Readiness: **on the curve, waiting for graduation**. |
| 3 | **Graduation at 85 SOL raised.** The curve completes when 85 SOL has been raised (`totalFundRaisingB` in `src/lib/burnticker/launchBundle.ts`). A deposit straight into the burn pool (the seed gift) is not curve funding and does not move this number. | LaunchLab: the pool status flips only when the fund-raising total is reached. | Coin page curve bar; the LaunchLab page. |
| 4 | **CPMM pool.** LaunchLab migrates the liquidity to a Raydium CPMM pool. | LaunchLab migration (`migrate_type` CPMM); the pool PDA derives from the platform's `cp_config`. | Coin page: the pool address once read. Readiness: **graduated, waiting for pool registration**. |
| 5 | **LP lock and Fee Key record.** 100 % of the graduation LP is locked with Raydium's lock; `register_fee_key` records the Fee Key (owned by the fee-keys PDA) for this pool. | `register_fee_key`: the Fee Key NFT is held by the fee-keys PDA with no delegate or close authority (`FeeKeyNotHeld`); the lock account must hold the majority of the LP (≥ 50 %, `FEE_KEY_MIN_LOCKED_LP_BPS`). | Coin page: "LP locked forever" block (lock program, Fee Key, owner). |
| 6 | **Pool registration and verification.** `register_pool` records the CPMM pool in the TokenConfig and marks it verified. | `register_pool`: pool creator = the fee PDA (`PoolCreatorMismatch`), the LaunchLab migration pool of the pinned platform (`NotMigrationPool`), LP ≥ 50 % locked (`LpNotLocked`), the FeeKeyRecord for this pool (`FeeKeyNotRegistered` / `FeeKeyMismatch`). `crank_buyback` refuses any coin without it: `require!(c.pool_verified, PoolNotVerified)`. | Coin page: program block "pool registered"; readiness leaves **waiting for pool registration**. |
| 7 | **A Treasury reading.** The keeper reads Treasury's Debt to the Penny for each business day; the attester quorum signs it into a Reading account. A day counts units only when the debt rose by at least one full $1B (remainders carry across consecutive rises). | `submit_reading`: valid, not future, not stale dates; non-zero source hash; the attester quorum (`QuorumNotMet`); a conflicting value for the same date is refused. | Coin page: readings and receipts; `deficit.burnticker.com` "today". Readiness: **waiting for a qualifying reading** while no reading has a unit. |
| 8 | **Budget at or above the spend floor.** The rule's spend for the day is `budget × (1 − (1 − 2 %)^units)` on the whole spendable burn pool (no burn-pool cap since the owner decision of 2026-10-25), plus any carried spend. | `crank_buyback`: a spend under the floor fixed at launch buys nothing and the pool keeps the SOL (outcome `below-floor`). | Receipt row "nothing bought — under the spend floor". Readiness: **reading recorded, budget below the spend floor**. |
| 9 | **crank_buyback.** The program buys $DEFICIT on its own registered pool and burns every token bought, in one instruction. | `crank_buyback`: the TWAP guard (`TwapDeviation`), the whole spend in one crank (no per-crank bound since 2026-10-25), the next reading in line, `NoUnits`. A deferral is a wait, with its reason. | Readiness: **execution deferred** with the program's reason, until the crank lands. |
| 10 | **Receipt.** The transaction's memo carries the reading, units, pool, spend, bought and burned amounts and the rule hash. | The memo is written by the program in the same instruction; `/burnticker/check` re-reads the transaction, the Reading and TokenConfig accounts and Treasury's data. | Coin page receipt; `/burnticker/check` (passed / failed / unavailable / not checked per check). Readiness: **burned and verified**. |

What this page does not say: nothing here describes what $DEFICIT trades at, and none of these steps has a date. Steps 2–3 depend on
trading on the curve; step 7 depends on Treasury's data. The current status of the platform itself (deployment, signing, upgrade
authority, open requirements) is in `GET /api/burnticker/v1/current-status` and on `/burnticker/trust`.
