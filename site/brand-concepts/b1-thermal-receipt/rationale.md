# B1 — Thermal receipt

The brand *is* the artefact the product produces: a thermal receipt. Paper is slightly grey-warm thermal stock with print-head
banding, one weak-dot stripe running the length of the slip and torn (serrated) ends; type is Courier Prime used as a receipt
(label left, value right, dashed rules), never as UI. The burned amount is literally burned out of the slip — an irregular scorch
hole with charred/brown/yellowed rings made with an SVG displacement filter, not a flame icon — and a violet rubber stamp with
uneven ink says BURNED ON-CHAIN. Palette: paper #f2eee4, thermal black #1b1916, stamp violet #5b3fa3, dark counter surface
behind. At 48 px the avatar reads as a pale slip with a dark hole on a dark ground; the header hangs three receipts from the
top edge (Treasury debt, a solar flare, a launch) so the launchpad idea — many feeds, one receipt format — is the picture.
What keeps it from looking generated: a real-world idiom carried through consistently, asymmetric tilts and lengths, texture
that comes from how thermal printers actually fail, and a joke (the hole) that only works in this idiom.
