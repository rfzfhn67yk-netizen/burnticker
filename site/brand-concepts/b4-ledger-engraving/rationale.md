# B4 — Ledger / engraving

The seriousness of a bond certificate: a register of supply reductions, numbered, ruled, engraved. The guilloche rosette is
generated as strokes — forty rotated ellipses and three families of sinusoidal wave rings, the way a geometric lathe draws
them — with a hatched engraving frame and a single gold hairline inside it. The wordmark is Playfair Display SC in true small
caps; figures are Playfair's old-style numerals with a hairline white relief so they sit "cut" into the paper. Palette: security
paper #e9e9df with a green-grey cast, engraver's green-black ink #15302a, gold #b8923f — not cream, not terracotta. The brand
mark is an ink cartouche with a gold B set on the rosette (at 48 px: a dark lozenge on a textured pale ground), numbered
No. 0001 in the corner; one corner of every sheet is burned away to the dark behind it, which is the only concession to the
word "burn". The header is the ledger itself: date / feed / reading / units / burned / receipt, three entries, rules between.
Why it does not read as generated: period-specific engraving construction (not a filter), small caps and old-style figures used
correctly, restraint — one ink, one metal, one italic — and a mark that is a cartouche, not a coin.
