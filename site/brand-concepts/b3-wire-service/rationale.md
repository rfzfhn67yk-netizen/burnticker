# B3 — Wire service / teletype

Burnticker as a newswire: it files dispatches, it does not sell. Archivo Black masthead (BURNTICKER WIRE, the WIRE in the one
signal red), Courier Prime body in all caps with wide tracking, a thick-thin rule system, a dateline with a reversed FLASH tag,
a slug line, and the old "— 30 —" / "— MORE —" end marks. The supply figure is struck through in red and restated, which is the
whole product in one typographic gesture. Along the top runs a strip of punched paper tape whose holes actually encode
BURNTICKER WIRE in 5-level ITA2 code, sprocket track in the right place — texture that is also true. Paper is a cool grey bond
(#e8e7e1), ink #141414, red #d9301c; a faint displacement filter gives the type the slightly struck, uneven edge of a typewriter
or teleprinter impression. The avatar stacks BURN / TICKER in the heavy grotesque so it reads as a black type block at 48 px.
Why it does not read as generated: monochrome plus one colour, left-ranged everything, period-correct editorial furniture
(dateline, slug, take number, end mark) and a code strip that is not decorative noise.
