# D2 — Treasury statement

$DEFICIT as the daily statement it is indexed to: a plain office document, not a government one. No seal, no eagle, no agency
name as a masthead — just the dataset cited (Debt to the Penny), a date, and the rows: TOTAL PUBLIC DEBT OUTSTANDING, prior
day, CHANGE, unit, units, BURN, with dotted leaders and tabular figures in IBM Plex Mono; the ticker and kicker in IBM Plex
Sans Condensed. The sheet has tri-fold creases, two ring-binder punches and a faint grain, and lies slightly askew on a dark
desk. The one colour is a rubber stamp: double-ring red, rotated, ink broken by a turbulence mask, placed over the change row
with the tx hash in its small line. Palette: sheet #f7f7f3, ink #161616, stamp red #c8102e, desk #2a2a29. At 48 px the avatar
is a white sheet with a red stamp mark — a document, not a logo. The header is the page seen close, punches at the left margin
where X's avatar sits, with the full arithmetic across the right. Why it does not read as generated: the humour is in the
deadpan (a statement that files its own burn), the type is used as accounting type, and the artefact details — creases,
punches, leaders, double-rule total — are the ones a real form has.
