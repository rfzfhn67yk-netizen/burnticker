Watch how a burn works. A real devnet burn, taken apart step by step.

  1. 1One locked ruleEach coin names one public result.

    Each coin names one public result, such as a game score or the national debt figure. The rule is locked when the coin launches.

  2. 2The result settlesWhen the result is published, the coin's own fees pay for its burn.

    When the result is published, fees from trading the coin buy back some of it from its pool.

    Before the first burn, the curve must raise the amount its creator chose (40, 60 or 85 SOL) from buyers, the coin must move to its Raydium pool, and that pool must be registered and verified by the burn program.

  3. 3Burned, with a receiptEverything bought is burned, and the transaction is the receipt.

    Everything bought is burned in the same transaction. That transaction is the receipt, and anyone can check it.

What these words mean
Memecoin
A speculative crypto token built around a theme.
Burn
Tokens the program destroys for good. It never takes tokens from anyone's wallet.
Receipt
The burn's transaction and the source reading behind it, so anyone can check the burn.
Settles
The source has published its final result for that period.
Trading fees
A small amount collected each time someone trades the coin.
Pool
The coin and SOL held by a trading program so that trades can happen.
Curve
A new coin's first trading stage. When buyers have put in the full raise, the coin graduates to its own Raydium pool.
Mint
The coin's unique address. A reserved address does not mean the coin exists yet.
Mainnet and devnet
Mainnet is the real Solana network, where transactions use real SOL. Devnet is a practice network whose tokens have no money value.
SOL and Raydium
SOL is Solana's own coin, used for fees and deposits. Raydium is the outside Solana trading service these coins trade on.

    Follow the season

    Each league gets one scoreboard per season, from the Argentine Primera to the WNBA. A board ranks that league's team coins by their receipts. Rivalries and the Burn Derby keep a running count of burns.

    The 25 leagues cover baseball, basketball, football, hockey and soccer. When a league's season ends, the coin with the most receipts is named its champion. Season titles are for the scoreboard only. There are no prizes.
