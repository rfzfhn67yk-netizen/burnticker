§5Verify it yourself

The rule file and its hash

The rule is fixed at launch and published as rules.json. Its SHA-256 is written into every burn's memo, so a receipt that cites a different hash is not this rule.

sha256: — (loaded from status.json)

Three steps, any machine

  1. Fetch the two Treasury rows a burn used — public API, no key: 
    curl -s 'https://api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov/services/api/fiscal_service/v2/accounting/od/debt_to_penny?fields=record_date,tot_pub_debt_out_amt&filter=record_date:in:(PREV_DATE,RECORD_DATE)&sort=record_date'
  2. Apply the rule: 
    units = floor((now − prev + carry) / 1,000,000,000)
spend = pool × (1 − 0.98^units)        # pool = the burn pool's SOL − 0.02 reserve
burn  = every token the swap delivered
    carry = the sub-$1B remainder from a run of consecutive increases; any down or flat day resets it to 0.
  3. Read the memo on the burn transaction — it carries the same numbers, in this order: 
    DEBT|2026-10-08|src=fiscaldata.treasury.gov debt_to_penny|prev=40284036147367.19|now=40305316210829.72|delta=21280063462.53|units=21|pool=…|spent=…|got=…|burn=…|swap=<sig12>|rules=sha256:dae4d8042ac1|v2
    If the memo, the chain and the Treasury rows agree, the receipt is good. The keeper recomputes every burn from its recorded readings and marks ✓ only when they match; anything else shows ! with the reason.

§6Who pays for the burn

1 · trading fees

Every trade of $DEFICIT pays a creator fee on the launch venue. That fee is the only income the mechanism has.

2 · the fee pool

Fees are claimed into the burn pool, an account of the on-chain program that has no withdraw instruction. Any seed deposit is disclosed with its transaction. Nobody can withdraw it; the program never sells.

3 · the swap

On a day with units the program spends 1 − 0.98units of the pool buying $DEFICIT on the token's Raydium CPMM pool. Under the floor (about $1) it buys nothing and the pool keeps it.

4 · the burn

Every token received is burned in the same instruction, with the memo. Holders' balances are never touched: no delegate, no transfer hook, no mint authority.

A memecoin. The rule says what supply does, not what price does.

$DEFICIT has no product, no revenue share and no team promise beyond running the published rule. Burning supply does not make a price go up; nobody here says it will. Buying fees fund the burns, so days with no trading are days with small burns.
